A DIY toaster made from cheap plastic (£4) and built entirely from scratch, involves extracting mica from an abandoned mine, melting copper in a microwave oven and turning potatoes into plastic. In the end it just creates a gobbled out mess of plastic which short circuits. This shows that even the simplest items in our daily lives require the invisible miracle of worldwide co-operation.
